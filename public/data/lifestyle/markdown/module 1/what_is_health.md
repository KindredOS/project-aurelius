## What is Health?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores the definition and dimensions of health. Prompt: What does it mean to be healthy in more than just a physical sense?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Health is more than not being sick. It includes your physical condition, how you feel emotionally, and how you think mentally. True health means feeling strong in your body, mind, and spirit, and being able to do the things you love with energy and joy.

## Physical Health

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section highlights the basics of physical health. Prompt: What are some simple habits that help your body stay healthy?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Physical health is about how well your body works. Eating nutritious food, getting enough sleep, moving your body, and staying hydrated all help keep your body strong. Regular checkups and hygiene also play a big role in physical wellness.

## Mental and Emotional Health

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section discusses the importance of thoughts and feelings. Prompt: How can your emotions affect your health?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Mental health involves your thoughts, stress levels, and mood. Emotional health includes how you handle feelings and relationships. Talking to someone you trust, expressing emotions, and practicing mindfulness all support a healthy mind.

## Social and Community Health

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains how your environment and relationships affect your health. Prompt: How do friendships and community make a difference in your well-being?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Good relationships help you feel supported and understood. Spending time with friends, family, and people who care about you boosts happiness and lowers stress. Safe, healthy communities also promote better living.

## Healthy Habits

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section focuses on creating and keeping healthy routines. Prompt: What is one small change you can make this week to improve your health?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Habits shape your health. Brushing your teeth, getting enough sleep, taking screen breaks, or going for a walk each day can make a big difference. Start small—consistent steps turn into lifelong routines.

## Preventive Care

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section looks at ways to prevent illness. Prompt: Why is it important to take care of your health even when you feel fine?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Preventive care means staying ahead of problems before they happen. That includes regular checkups, vaccines, and healthy choices like wearing sunscreen or washing your hands. Prevention saves time, money, and pain.

## Health and Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how new tools are helping people stay healthy. Prompt: How do apps or devices help you track or improve your health?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Today, technology plays a big role in health. Fitness trackers, sleep apps, and telehealth services make it easier to learn about your body and talk to doctors. These tools help people make smart, healthy decisions every day.

## Personal Health Goals

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section encourages reflection on personal wellness. Prompt: What is one health goal you have for yourself, and how can you work toward it?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Setting goals helps you grow. Whether it’s drinking more water, reducing stress, or being more active, your goals should be realistic and meaningful to you. Tracking progress and celebrating small wins keeps you motivated.
