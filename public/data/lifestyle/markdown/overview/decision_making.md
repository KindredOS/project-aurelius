## What is Decision-Making?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What kinds of decisions do you make every day, and how do they affect your life?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Decision-making is the process of choosing between different options. Some choices are small, like what to wear, while others are big, like how to spend your time. Each decision shapes your lifestyle, your future, and who you become.

## Values-Based Choices

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What values are most important to you, and how do they guide your choices?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

When we know what matters most to us—like honesty, health, or kindness—we can use those values to guide our decisions. Values-based choices help us stay true to ourselves.

## Thinking Ahead

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How can thinking about future consequences help you make better choices?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Good decision-making involves looking ahead. Asking "What might happen next?" helps us avoid regret and make choices that support our goals.

## The Role of Emotions

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do your feelings affect the way you make decisions?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Emotions give us helpful information, but they can also cloud our thinking. Learning to notice emotions without letting them take over helps us make balanced, thoughtful choices.

## Critical Thinking

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What does it mean to think critically, and how can it help with problem-solving?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Critical thinking means asking questions, checking facts, and considering different perspectives. It helps us avoid being tricked or misled and leads to smarter, more informed decisions.
