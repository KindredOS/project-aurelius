## What is Lifestyle?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What does "lifestyle" mean, and how does it affect who we are?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Lifestyle refers to the daily choices, habits, and environments that shape how we live. These include what we eat, how we spend our time, where we live, and how we interact with others. Our lifestyle choices influence our health, relationships, values, and sense of identity.

## Habits and Routines

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What are some daily habits that help create a healthy lifestyle?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Our routines, like brushing our teeth, getting enough sleep, or setting aside time for homework, all shape our lifestyle. Healthy habits add up over time and help us build structure, reduce stress, and improve well-being.

\[interactive element]

## Food and Nutrition

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do our food choices influence our energy and mood each day?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

What we eat fuels our body and brain. Choosing a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and proteins helps us stay alert, active, and emotionally balanced. Unhealthy eating can lead to low energy or illness over time.

## Movement and Exercise

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What kinds of movement do you enjoy, and how do they help your body?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Lifestyle includes how active we are. Whether it's dancing, biking, walking, or playing sports, regular movement strengthens our muscles, supports mental health, and keeps our hearts healthy.

## Relationships and Belonging

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do your friendships and family shape your lifestyle?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

The people we spend time with influence how we think, behave, and feel. Supportive relationships make us feel safe, valued, and confident. Sharing experiences builds connection and a sense of belonging.

## Environment and Place

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How does where you live or spend time affect your lifestyle?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Our surroundings, like home, school, or neighborhood, shape what we can do and how we feel. A clean, safe, and inspiring environment can help us focus, relax, or feel motivated.

## Values and Identity

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What personal values guide your decisions each day?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Our lifestyle reflects what we care about. If we value kindness, we may help others. If we value learning, we might read or explore. Over time, our choices reveal and shape our identity.

\[interactive element]

## Balancing Lifestyle

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What makes a balanced lifestyle, and how can you find it?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

A good lifestyle balances work, play, rest, and connection. It means making time for health, growth, and joy. Balance helps us avoid burnout and keeps us feeling fulfilled in everyday life.
