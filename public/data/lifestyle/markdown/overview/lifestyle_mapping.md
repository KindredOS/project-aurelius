## What is Lifestyle Mapping?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What parts of your daily life influence your overall lifestyle?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Lifestyle mapping is the process of identifying the choices, habits, people, and environments that affect how we live. By mapping these out, we can see the patterns that influence our health, emotions, and goals. It helps us make more intentional choices.

## Systems That Shape Us

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What systems (like school, family, or media) play a role in your lifestyle?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

We are influenced by many systems—like school rules, family routines, community values, or online culture. These systems affect how we behave and what we believe. Understanding them helps us recognize both support and pressure in our lives.

## Personal Habits Inventory

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What are three habits you have that support your well-being? What’s one you’d like to change?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Reflecting on habits can show what’s working and what needs adjusting. This could include sleep routines, eating patterns, screen time, or how we manage stress. Awareness is the first step toward change.

\[interactive element]

## Emotional Check-In

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do different parts of your lifestyle make you feel emotionally?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Emotions often point to lifestyle factors that may need attention. Feeling energized or stressed, confident or overwhelmed, can relate to sleep, relationships, or responsibilities. Noticing our emotions helps us tune into what matters.

\[interactive element]

## Time and Energy

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: Where does your time and energy go each week? What would you change?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Mapping how we spend our time shows our priorities. It also reveals imbalances—like too much screen time or too little rest. Adjusting how we use our energy can lead to healthier, more balanced living.

## External Influences

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do outside influences like friends, trends, or media affect your lifestyle?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Friends, influencers, trends, and ads all shape our choices. Being aware of these influences helps us decide which ones support or distract from our values and goals.

## Vision for Change

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: If you could change one part of your lifestyle, what would it be and why?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Mapping helps us set goals and imagine new possibilities. Even small changes can create big impacts over time. Having a vision makes it easier to take meaningful steps toward the life we want.

\[interactive element]

## Reflect, Revise, Repeat

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How can regular reflection help you shape a lifestyle that supports you?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Reflection is a tool for growth. Checking in regularly helps us see progress and adapt when needed. Life changes—and our lifestyle can too. Reflection gives us the power to guide that change.
