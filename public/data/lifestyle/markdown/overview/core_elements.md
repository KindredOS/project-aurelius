## What Are Core Elements?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What are the most important building blocks of a healthy lifestyle, and why do they matter?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Core elements are the essential parts of a healthy lifestyle. These include physical health, mindset, relationships, energy, and purpose. When these areas are strong and balanced, we feel more fulfilled and able to thrive.

## Physical Health

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What do you do to take care of your body, and how does it affect your daily life?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Good health starts with taking care of our bodies through nutrition, exercise, sleep, and hygiene. A healthy body supports focus, mood, and energy—helping us do our best each day.

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How does your attitude affect your ability to handle challenges?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Our mindset shapes how we see the world and respond to problems. A positive, flexible mindset helps us learn from mistakes, stay motivated, and manage stress more effectively.

\[Prompt Wrap End]

\[Prompt Wrap End]

\[Prompt Wrap End]
