## Hands-On Activities

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What can we learn by making things with our own hands?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Hands-on art activities let students explore their creativity while building real-world skills. These projects help learners express themselves, collaborate with others, and better understand artistic tools and techniques. Making things by hand builds focus, problem-solving, and confidence.

## Self-Portraits

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How can drawing or painting yourself help you learn more about who you are?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Self-portraits allow students to reflect on identity, emotions, and style. They can experiment with expression, symbolism, and technique to represent themselves in unique ways. This activity helps students explore how art can communicate who we are and how we feel.

## Zine Making

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What story or message would you want to share in your own mini-magazine?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Zines are small, handmade booklets filled with images, words, and ideas. Students can use them to share stories, opinions, poetry, comics, or collages. Zine making blends writing, design, and visual art, giving students the freedom to create and publish their voice.

## Public Art

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How can art in public spaces make people feel more connected?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Public art includes murals, sculptures, and installations in community spaces. Students can design artwork that reflects their neighborhood, school, or culture. This type of project shows how art can bring people together and start conversations in shared environments.

## Reimagining Famous Artworks

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What happens when you take a famous painting and give it your own twist?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Students reinterpret classic pieces like the Mona Lisa or Starry Night in a modern or personal style. This helps them learn about art history while practicing creative thinking. They might change the colors, add new elements, or transform the setting entirely.

## Mixed Media Projects

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What happens when you combine different materials into one piece of art?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Mixed media projects encourage experimentation with paper, fabric, found objects, paint, and more. These activities teach students to think flexibly and work with texture, contrast, and composition. There's no single "right way" to make mixed media—just imagination and intention.

## Wearable Art

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How can clothing or accessories become a form of artistic expression?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Students create items like painted T-shirts, handmade jewelry, or costume pieces that show off their personality and design skills. Wearable art connects fashion and function with creativity. It teaches about materials, color theory, and cultural expression.

## Community Story Quilt

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What would your quilt square say about your story or background?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Each student designs a square that tells a personal or family story using images, patterns, or words. The squares are then assembled into one large quilt. This collaborative project builds connection, celebrates diversity, and shows how individual stories create a shared narrative.
