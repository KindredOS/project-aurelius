## Art and Mental Health

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How can making or viewing art support emotional well-being?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Creating or observing art can be calming, joyful, and healing. Art therapy helps people express feelings they can’t put into words. Colors, shapes, and images can unlock emotions, build confidence, and offer comfort in times of stress or change.
