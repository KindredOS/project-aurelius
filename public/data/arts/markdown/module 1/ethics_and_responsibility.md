## Ethics & Responsibility in Visual Arts

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What does it mean to be a responsible artist?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Being a responsible artist means thinking about how your work affects others. It involves being honest, respectful, and fair in how you create, share, and use art. Good artists give credit where it’s due, ask permission when needed, and avoid harmful stereotypes.

## Copyright and Fair Use

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Why is it important to respect copyright in visual art?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Copyright protects original art so creators can control how it's used. Copying someone else's work without permission is not only unfair—it can be illegal. Fair use allows limited use for education or commentary, but it's best to ask or create something new.

## Cultural Appropriation

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What is cultural appropriation, and why is it a problem in art?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Cultural appropriation is when someone uses elements from a culture they don’t belong to without understanding or respect. It can feel like stealing, especially if the culture has been marginalized. Respectful artists learn about cultures and collaborate instead of copying.

## Giving Credit

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How can artists properly credit sources or inspirations?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

If your work is inspired by someone else’s idea, image, or style, it’s important to say so. You can include a note, tag the creator, or list them in your project description. Giving credit builds trust and shows appreciation for others’ work.

## Representation and Stereotypes

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How can visual art challenge or reinforce stereotypes?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Art has the power to shape how people see each other. Thoughtful artists avoid repeating harmful cliches and instead try to represent people honestly and with care. Asking for feedback from diverse voices helps make work more inclusive and fair.

## Consent and Collaboration

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Why should artists get permission when including others in their work?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Using someone’s image, story, or idea without asking can cross personal boundaries. Getting consent shows respect and builds trust. In collaborations, it’s also key to agree on roles, credit, and how the work will be shared.

## Ethics in Digital Art

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What special responsibilities come with creating and sharing digital art?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Digital art can spread fast and be easily copied or changed. Responsible artists use watermarks, credit others, and avoid spreading misinformation. They also think about how filters, edits, and AI tools affect truth and originality.

## Your Role as an Artist

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What values do you want your art to reflect?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Art is a powerful voice. As an artist, you get to decide what you stand for. You can choose to be respectful, curious, inclusive, and honest. Thinking about your impact makes your art stronger and more meaningful to others.
