## Mechanical Engineering

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains what mechanical engineers do.
**Prompt:** What machines or devices do you use every day that a mechanical engineer might design?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Mechanical engineers work with machines, engines, and moving parts. They design things like cars, airplanes, tools, and robots. Their work helps things move, run smoothly, and last longer.
