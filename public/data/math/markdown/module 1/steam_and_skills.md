## What Are STEAM Skills?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces STEAM and the skills it helps build. Prompt: What kinds of skills do you think you build by learning science, technology, engineering, arts, and math?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

STEAM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Math. These subjects teach us to ask questions, solve problems, think creatively, and use logic. Learning STEAM helps you become a strong thinker and doer.

## Problem Solving

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section highlights problem-solving as a core STEAM skill. Prompt: What steps do you take when you face a difficult problem?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

In STEAM, you learn to approach problems with a plan. Break it into parts, think of solutions, test them, and try again if needed. Every failure is a chance to learn something new.

## Creativity and Design

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how creativity plays a role in STEAM fields. Prompt: How do art and imagination help with science and engineering?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

STEAM is not just about numbers and tools—it’s also about imagination! Artists and engineers both design, invent, and create. Whether it’s drawing, coding, or building, creativity brings ideas to life.

## Collaboration

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section focuses on working with others to solve problems. Prompt: Why is teamwork important in STEAM?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Most STEAM projects are done in teams. That’s because different people bring different strengths. Working together helps you listen, share ideas, and build better solutions than you could alone.

## Critical Thinking

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains how to think deeply and ask good questions. Prompt: Why is it important to ask questions and look at all the details?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Critical thinking means not just accepting answers but digging deeper. You ask "why?" and "how?" You look at the facts and decide what makes the most sense. This is how scientists and inventors think.

## Communication

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains why sharing your ideas clearly is part of STEAM. Prompt: How do you explain your thinking to someone else?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Being able to explain your ideas is just as important as having them. In STEAM, you learn to talk, write, or draw your thinking so others can understand and build on your work.

\[interactive element]

## Perseverance

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section shows how STEAM encourages a growth mindset. Prompt: What do you do when something is hard but you want to get better at it?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

In STEAM, mistakes are part of learning. You try, you fail, you learn, and you try again. Sticking with a challenge even when it’s tough helps you grow smarter and stronger.

## Real-Life Connections

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section shows how STEAM skills are used in everyday life and careers. Prompt: Can you think of a job that uses science, technology, engineering, art, or math?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

From doctors and designers to builders and coders, STEAM skills are everywhere. When you learn STEAM, you’re preparing for exciting jobs and real-world challenges in your future.
