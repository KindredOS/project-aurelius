## Why Hands-On Learning Matters

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces the value of learning through doing. Prompt: Why do you think doing an activity helps you understand a topic better?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

## Math in Motion

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section connects physical movement and math learning. Prompt: How can moving around help you understand a math problem?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Sometimes moving your body helps your brain understand. You can walk a number line, jump out equations, or act out math stories. It turns abstract ideas into something you can see and feel.

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section shows how building helps explore math and science. Prompt: What can you learn by building a model, like a bridge or a cube?

\[Prompt Wrap End]
