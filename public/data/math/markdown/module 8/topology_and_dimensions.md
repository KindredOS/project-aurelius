## Topology and Higher Dimensions

---

### What Is Topology?

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces topology as the study of shapes and spaces that can bend or stretch.  
Prompt: How is a coffee mug like a donut in topology?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

**Topology** is the study of properties of shapes that stay the same when stretched or bent, but not when cut or glued.  
- A coffee mug and a donut are the same in topology (each has one hole).  
- Shapes are compared by their fundamental structure, not size or angles.

### Surfaces and Holes

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explores surfaces and connectedness.  
Prompt: How can two shapes be different just because of how many holes they have?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Topology classifies objects by features like:  
- Number of holes (torus vs. sphere).  
- Boundaries (disk vs. circle).  
- Connectedness (one piece vs. multiple parts).  

These properties define the “essence” of a shape.

### Higher Dimensions

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces spaces beyond three dimensions.  
Prompt: What would it be like to imagine a cube in four dimensions?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

We live in three dimensions, but math explores more:  
- A **tesseract** is a 4D cube.  
- Higher dimensions appear in physics and data science.  
- Projections help us visualize shapes beyond our perception.  

Higher dimensions expand our imagination of space.

### Topology in Action

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explains how topology is applied.  
Prompt: Why might mathematicians study shapes that can bend and stretch?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

- Computer graphics: deforming objects smoothly.  
- Robotics: mapping possible motions of a robot arm.  
- Biology: modeling DNA as twisted loops.  
- Physics: studying spacetime and black holes.  

Topology is abstract but surprisingly practical.
