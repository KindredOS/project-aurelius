### What Is Quantum Math?

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces quantum math as the language of the microscopic world.  
Prompt: How is predicting an electron’s position different from predicting a ball’s path?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

**Quantum math** describes particles that behave in surprising ways—sometimes like waves, sometimes like particles. Instead of exact certainty, it deals with probabilities of where or how something might be observed.

### Probability at the Core

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explains why quantum systems are probabilistic.  
Prompt: Why can’t we say exactly where an electron is until we measure it?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

In quantum mechanics, probabilities replace certainty.  
- The **wave function** gives the likelihood of different outcomes.  
- Measurement “collapses” possibilities into one result.  

This makes probability central to quantum thinking.

### Superposition and Entanglement

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces strange quantum behaviors.  
Prompt: How can a particle be in two states at once—or instantly connected to another far away?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

- **Superposition**: a particle can exist in multiple states until measured.  
- **Entanglement**: particles share states, even across distance.  

These behaviors challenge our classical ideas of reality.

### Quantum Math in Action

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section connects quantum probability to applications.  
Prompt: How might quantum rules make computers more powerful?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

- **Quantum computing** uses qubits in superposition to perform massive calculations.  
- **Quantum cryptography** secures communication using entanglement and uncertainty.  
- **Quantum models** help explain chemistry and new materials.  

Math drives these new technologies.
