## Money and Budgeting

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section shows how math helps us manage money.  
Prompt: How do you figure out if you have enough money to buy something?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

We use arithmetic and algebra to track spending, set budgets, and calculate savings.  
- Equations help with interest on savings or loans.  
- Inequalities model limits like “I can’t spend more than \$50.”  

Financial literacy depends on math.

## Travel and Navigation

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section connects math to movement and planning.  
Prompt: How can math help you estimate how long a trip will take?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Travel uses math all the time:  
- Distance = rate × time.  
- Maps use scale to shrink large distances.  
- GPS relies on geometry and coordinates.  

Math helps us get from one place to another efficiently.

## Design and Construction

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section highlights geometry in the built world.  
Prompt: Where do you think builders and architects use math?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Construction and design depend on geometry and measurement.  
- Perimeter and area for floor plans.  
- Volume for materials.  
- Angles for stability and fit.  

Without math, we couldn’t build safe structures or design efficient spaces.
