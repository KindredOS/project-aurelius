## Growth vs. Fixed Mindset

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section compares two ways of thinking about learning. Prompt: What does it mean to have a growth mindset in math?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

A fixed mindset says you're either "good" at math or you're not. A growth mindset says you can improve through hard work, strategies, and help from others. The way you think about challenges shapes your success.
