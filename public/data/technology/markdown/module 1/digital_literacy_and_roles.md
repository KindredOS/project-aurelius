## What is Digital Literacy?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What do you think it means to be "digitally literate"?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Digital literacy is the ability to use technology safely, responsibly, and effectively. It means knowing how to find information online, use digital tools, and think critically about what you see on the internet. Being digitally literate helps you learn, connect, and stay safe in the digital world.

## Using Digital Tools

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What digital tools do you use in school or at home?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

From tablets and laptops to learning apps and search engines, digital tools are everywhere. Learning how to use them well helps you research, collaborate, create, and communicate. It's about more than just using a device—it's about using it with purpose.

## Online Safety and Citizenship

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How can you stay safe and respectful online?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Being a good digital citizen means protecting your privacy, treating others with kindness, and avoiding risky behavior online. That includes creating strong passwords, thinking before you click, and knowing when to ask for help.

## Evaluating Information Online

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How do you know if something online is true or trustworthy?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

The internet has lots of great info—but also false or misleading content. Learn to check sources, compare websites, and ask questions. Digital literacy means thinking like a detective: looking for clues to decide what’s fact or fiction.

## Digital Communication

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What are some good ways to communicate with others online?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Email, chats, and video calls are common ways to connect. Knowing how to communicate clearly, respectfully, and safely helps you share ideas, work in teams, and build friendships. Emojis can help—but so can good spelling and kind words.

## Roles in the Digital World

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What kinds of jobs or tasks require digital literacy?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

From graphic design to data entry, many careers involve digital skills. Even students have roles—like being a group tech leader or helping classmates use a new app. Understanding your role helps you contribute and grow.

## Rights and Responsibilities

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What responsibilities do you have when using the internet?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Being online comes with both rights and responsibilities. You have the right to access information and express yourself, but also the responsibility to respect others, avoid plagiarism, and follow rules. Good digital citizens lead by example.
