## What is a System?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What does it mean to think of your lifestyle as a system?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

A system is a group of connected parts that work together. When we think of lifestyle as a system, we recognize that our routines, habits, choices, and environments all interact. Each part affects the whole.

## Inputs and Outputs

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What are some inputs (like food, sleep, or screen time) that affect your daily energy and mood?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Inputs are the things we put into our lives—like rest, food, exercise, media, and relationships. Outputs are what result—like focus, mood, creativity, or stress. Good inputs lead to better outcomes.

\[interactive element]

## Feedback Loops

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: Have you ever noticed how one habit can lead to another—good or bad?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

In a system, feedback loops show how one action can reinforce others. A good night's sleep can lead to better focus, which helps with schoolwork, which boosts confidence. Small changes can create big ripple effects.

\[interactive element]

## Interconnected Parts

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How are your health, friendships, and daily choices connected?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your life is made of many parts—physical health, mental state, relationships, learning, and more. These parts are linked. When one part improves or declines, it often affects others.

## Environmental Influence

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How does your environment—like your home, school, or neighborhood—shape your lifestyle system?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Our environment plays a big role in shaping our habits. A cluttered room can make it harder to focus. Supportive spaces encourage growth. Design your surroundings to support your goals.

## Small Changes, Big Impact

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What is one small change you could make that might improve your lifestyle system?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Because all parts of your lifestyle are connected, one small shift—like going to bed earlier or spending less time on your phone—can lead to positive changes across the system.

## Systems Thinking Skills

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: Why is it helpful to step back and look at the big picture of your life?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Systems thinking means zooming out and observing how everything connects. It helps you identify patterns, understand cause and effect, and plan more effectively for the future.

## Designing Your System

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: If you could redesign one part of your lifestyle system, what would it be and why?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Thinking in systems gives you power to create change. By identifying what’s working and what’s not, you can make intentional adjustments that lead to a more balanced, healthy, and rewarding lifestyle.
