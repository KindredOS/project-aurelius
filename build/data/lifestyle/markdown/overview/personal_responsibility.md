## What is Personal Responsibility?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What does it mean to be responsible for your actions and choices?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Personal responsibility means owning our actions and their consequences. It includes doing what we say we will do, making thoughtful choices, and learning from mistakes. Being responsible builds trust and helps us grow into capable individuals.

\[Prompt Wrap Start]

## The Role of Forgiveness

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: Why is it important to forgive others—and yourself?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Forgiveness is a powerful part of emotional health. When we let go of resentment or guilt, we make space for healing and growth. Forgiving doesn't mean forgetting—it means choosing peace over anger.

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
