## What is Lifestyle Design?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What does it mean to "design" your life? How is it different from just going with the flow?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Lifestyle design means making intentional choices to shape how you live—based on your values, goals, and needs. Instead of letting habits or outside forces decide for you, you become the architect of your daily life.

## Vision and Values

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What are some values that matter most to you? How could your lifestyle reflect them?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Designing your lifestyle starts with knowing what’s important to you. Do you value creativity, health, or community? Aligning your actions with your values leads to a more meaningful and satisfying life.

## Goal Setting

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What’s a goal you’d like to achieve this year? What steps could help you get there?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Setting goals helps give your life direction. Whether it's improving your grades, learning a skill, or getting more sleep, breaking goals into small, doable steps makes success more likely.

## Routine Building

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What routines do you have—or want to build—that support your well-being?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Routines make healthy behaviors easier to repeat. Morning stretches, nightly reading, or weekly cleanups help reduce stress and boost focus. Designing routines helps make good choices automatic.

## Energy Management

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: When do you feel most energized during the day? How can you use that time well?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Everyone has energy highs and lows. Learning your patterns helps you plan your day better—doing harder tasks when you're alert and saving simpler ones for when you're tired.

## Time Architecture

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What does your weekly schedule look like now? What would you change if you could?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Lifestyle design includes shaping your schedule to match your priorities. This might mean cutting back on distractions, making time for creativity, or protecting time to rest and recharge.

## Feedback and Flexibility

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What’s something in your life that you’ve adjusted after seeing it didn’t work well?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Good design includes testing and tweaking. If something isn’t working—like a new sleep routine or study method—adjust it. Flexibility lets your lifestyle evolve as you grow.
