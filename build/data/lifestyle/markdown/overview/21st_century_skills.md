## What Are 21st Century Skills?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What skills do you think are most important for success in today’s world?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

21st century skills include creativity, collaboration, communication, and critical thinking. They also involve self-awareness, adaptability, emotional intelligence, and digital literacy. These skills help us thrive in a fast-changing world.

\[interactive element]

## Self-Awareness

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What are some of your strengths, and how do they help you learn or grow?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Self-awareness means understanding your thoughts, emotions, and habits. It helps you recognize your strengths and challenges so you can make better choices and grow with confidence.

\[interactive element]

## Adaptability

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: When was a time you had to adjust to something new? What helped you succeed?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Being adaptable means staying flexible when things change. It helps you handle challenges, bounce back from setbacks, and stay open to new ideas and situations.

\[interactive element]

## Emotional Intelligence

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do you recognize and manage your emotions in tough situations?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Emotional intelligence is the ability to understand and manage your own emotions—and recognize the emotions of others. It supports healthy communication, empathy, and teamwork.

## Lifelong Learning

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What’s something new you’ve learned recently, and how did it feel?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Lifelong learning means staying curious and always being open to new skills and knowledge. It helps us grow personally and professionally, no matter our age or background.

## Collaboration

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What helps you work well with others on a team?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Working well with others means listening, sharing ideas, and helping the group reach a goal. Collaboration builds trust, solves problems, and brings out everyone’s strengths.

\[interactive element]

## Digital Literacy

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do you use technology responsibly and effectively in your daily life?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Digital literacy includes knowing how to use technology for learning, creating, and communicating. It also means using it wisely, safely, and with respect for others.

\[interactive element]

## Growth Through Feedback

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do you respond when someone gives you helpful feedback?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Feedback helps us improve. Learning to accept and use feedback—with a positive attitude—makes us stronger learners and team members.
