## Elements of Health

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces the core building blocks of good health. Prompt: What are the most important things your body and mind need to stay healthy?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Health isn’t just about avoiding sickness—it’s about giving your body and mind what they need to thrive. Key elements include eating well, staying active, sleeping enough, drinking water, spending time in sunlight, and managing emotions. These building blocks support a strong and balanced life.

## Nutrition

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section highlights the role of food as fuel. Prompt: How does the food you eat affect your energy, focus, and growth?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Nutritious food powers your body and brain. Eating a variety of fruits, vegetables, proteins, and whole grains gives you energy and helps you grow. Healthy eating habits also support mood and focus throughout the day.

\[interactive element]

## Sleep

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section focuses on the value of rest and repair. Prompt: Why is getting enough sleep important for your health?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Sleep gives your body and brain time to recover and recharge. It helps with learning, mood, memory, and even your immune system. Good sleep habits help you feel more alert and ready to learn.

\[interactive element]

## Exercise

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section emphasizes the importance of movement. Prompt: What are some ways you can move your body that are fun and healthy?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Movement keeps your heart strong, your muscles active, and your mind clear. Exercise doesn’t have to mean sports—dancing, walking, biking, or even stretching count. Moving each day helps keep you feeling energized and positive.

\[interactive element]

## Hydration

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains why water is vital. Prompt: How does staying hydrated help your body and brain?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Water helps every part of your body work better. It keeps your cells healthy, your temperature balanced, and your brain focused. Drinking enough water each day is one of the simplest ways to take care of yourself.

\[interactive element]

## Sunlight and Nature

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section describes the benefits of spending time outdoors. Prompt: How do sunshine and nature help your mood or energy?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Sunlight helps your body make vitamin D and supports healthy sleep rhythms. Being outside in nature can also reduce stress and lift your mood. Just a few minutes of sunlight each day can make a big difference.

\[interactive element]

## Emotional Regulation

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how emotions affect health. Prompt: What are some healthy ways to deal with big emotions like anger or sadness?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your emotions are part of your health too. Learning to notice, name, and express feelings helps you handle stress and build strong relationships. Talking to someone, writing in a journal, or taking deep breaths are good ways to stay emotionally healthy.

\[interactive element]

## Putting It All Together

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section encourages students to combine healthy habits into a routine. Prompt: What are three healthy habits you already practice, and one you want to improve?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Good health comes from combining small habits that support your whole self. A routine that includes movement, rest, water, good food, and emotional care helps you grow strong and feel your best. Start with what works for you and build from there.
