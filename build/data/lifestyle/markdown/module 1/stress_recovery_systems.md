## Stress and Recovery Systems

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces the body’s stress and recovery systems. Prompt: What happens to your body when you feel stressed, and how does it recover?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

When you face a challenge or danger, your body reacts with a stress response. Your heart races, breathing quickens, and energy spikes. This is called "fight or flight." After the challenge passes, your body needs time to relax and recover. Understanding this balance helps you stay healthy.

## The Autonomic Nervous System

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains how the autonomic nervous system manages stress and recovery. Prompt: What are the two main parts of your autonomic nervous system, and what do they do?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your autonomic nervous system works without you thinking about it. It has two key parts: the sympathetic system, which prepares your body to deal with stress, and the parasympathetic system, which helps you calm down and heal. Both systems are always working to keep your body balanced.

## Physical Signs of Stress

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how stress shows up in your body. Prompt: What are some ways your body tells you it’s stressed?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Stress doesn’t just affect the mind—it also shows up in the body. You might get headaches, tense muscles, an upset stomach, or feel very tired. Learning to notice these signs early can help you take action before stress builds up.

## Emotional and Mental Effects

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section describes how stress affects thoughts and feelings. Prompt: How can stress change the way you think or feel?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Stress can make you feel overwhelmed, anxious, or irritated. It can be hard to focus or make decisions. These mental and emotional effects are just as important to understand as physical signs. Talking about your feelings and getting support is key to managing stress.

## Healthy Recovery Practices

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section focuses on practices that support recovery. Prompt: What activities help you feel calm and reset after a stressful day?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Recovery is how your body and mind return to balance. Things like sleep, deep breathing, walking in nature, or talking to a friend can activate your calming system. Regular recovery practices keep stress from building up over time.

## The Role of Sleep

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section highlights the connection between sleep and stress recovery. Prompt: How does sleep help your body recover from stress?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Sleep is one of the best ways to recover from stress. While you sleep, your brain sorts emotions, your muscles relax, and your body repairs itself. Good sleep habits help your mind stay sharp and your mood stay balanced.

## Mindfulness and Relaxation

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces mindfulness as a recovery tool. Prompt: What is mindfulness, and how can it help with stress?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Mindfulness means paying full attention to the present moment. It helps quiet racing thoughts and brings awareness to your body and feelings. Even a few minutes of breathing or mindful movement can activate your body’s recovery mode.

## Building a Personal Recovery Plan

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section encourages students to create their own stress recovery toolkit. Prompt: What are three things you can do when you start to feel stressed?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Everyone handles stress differently, so it helps to make a recovery plan that works for you. Think about what calms you, where you feel safe, and who you can talk to. Having a plan means you’re ready to take care of yourself when challenges come.
