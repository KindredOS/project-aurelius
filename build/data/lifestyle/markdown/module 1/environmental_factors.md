## Environmental Factors

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces how surroundings affect well-being. Prompt: How does your environment affect your mood, energy, or focus?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your environment includes the sounds, lights, and digital tools around you. Bright lights or loud noises can be stressful, while quiet, natural spaces help you feel calm. Being aware of your surroundings helps you make better choices for focus and rest.

## Light and Mood

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how lighting affects how you feel. Prompt: How do different types of light make you feel during the day?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Natural sunlight helps your body feel awake and happy. Dim or blue light, especially from screens, can make you tired or mess with your sleep. Getting sunlight during the day and turning off screens at night supports healthy rhythms.

## Noise and Focus

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section discusses the role of sound in focus and calm. Prompt: What types of sounds help you concentrate or relax?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Noise affects your brain. Loud or random sounds can make it hard to concentrate. Calm music or white noise can help you focus or rest. Learning to control your sound environment gives your brain a break.

## Digital Exposure

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section addresses the effects of screen time and devices. Prompt: How do digital devices help or hurt your daily life?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Screens can be helpful for learning and connecting, but too much can cause tired eyes, poor sleep, or stress. Setting limits, taking breaks, and using screens in a smart way helps keep things in balance.

## Media and Mindset

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains how media content shapes thoughts and feelings. Prompt: How does what you watch, read, or listen to affect your mindset?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Media can inspire or overwhelm. What you watch, read, or listen to can shape how you feel and think. Positive, thoughtful media helps your brain grow; too much negativity can bring stress. Choose wisely for a healthy mindset.

## Creating Calm Spaces

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section encourages building environments that support calm. Prompt: What makes a space feel calm, and how can you create one?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

A calm space might have soft light, gentle sounds, plants, or a cozy chair. Creating a peaceful spot helps you relax and recharge. Everyone needs a quiet corner sometimes—even if it’s just a blanket and headphones.

## Environment and Energy

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section looks at how surroundings affect physical energy. Prompt: What changes in your environment help you feel more energized?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your space can give or take energy. Fresh air, sunlight, and tidy areas boost energy. Messy, dark, or noisy spots can feel draining. Making small changes to your space can lift your mood and get you moving.

## Designing a Healthier Space

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section helps students reflect on their own spaces. Prompt: What is one change you could make to your room or workspace to support your well-being?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

You can shape your space to support your health. Add color, light, plants, or a schedule. Organize tools you use every day. Even a small change can make your room feel like a better place to focus or relax.
