## Lifestyle Implementation

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces healthy habit-building. Prompt: What does a healthy lifestyle mean to you?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

A healthy lifestyle means making choices that help your body and mind thrive. These choices include what you eat, how you move, how much you sleep, and how you handle stress. The key is being consistent with small actions that lead to big changes over time.

## Morning Routines

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how to start the day with purpose. Prompt: What could you do each morning to help your day go better?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

A strong morning routine sets the tone for the day. Stretching, eating a good breakfast, reviewing your goals, or taking a few deep breaths can help you feel prepared and positive. Morning habits create structure and build momentum.

## Meal Planning and Nutrition

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section highlights the role of food in energy and focus. Prompt: How can planning your meals help you stay healthy and focused?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Healthy meals fuel your brain and body. Planning ahead helps you make better choices and avoid skipping meals or grabbing junk food. Balanced meals with fruits, vegetables, protein, and whole grains support growth and focus.

## Movement and Exercise

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section emphasizes the importance of regular activity. Prompt: What kinds of movement do you enjoy that help your body stay active?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Staying active keeps your heart strong and your mood lifted. You don’t have to play sports—dancing, walking the dog, biking, or playing tag all count. Finding fun ways to move makes it easier to stick with.

## Energy Management

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section teaches how to balance activity and rest. Prompt: How can you manage your energy during busy or stressful days?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Energy isn’t unlimited, so learning to manage it matters. Short breaks, healthy snacks, staying hydrated, and getting good sleep all help. Listening to your body and pacing yourself can keep you feeling strong all day.

## Tech-Life Balance

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section encourages thoughtful use of technology. Prompt: How can screen time affect your energy, sleep, or mood?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Technology is useful but can also be distracting or draining. Too much screen time—especially before bed—can affect sleep and focus. Setting screen-free times and using tech for learning or creativity helps create a better balance.

## Tracking Progress

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section focuses on reflection and small wins. Prompt: How can you tell if your healthy habits are working?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Tracking progress helps you stay motivated. A journal, checklist, or mood chart can show how habits are helping. Even small changes—like more energy or better sleep—mean you're moving in the right direction.

\[interactive element]

## Creating a Lifestyle Plan

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section guides students to build their own plan. Prompt: What is one new habit you want to try this week, and how will you stick with it?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Making a plan helps turn good ideas into real change. Choose a small, doable habit like drinking more water or getting outside every day. Write it down, set a reminder, and ask a friend to cheer you on. Success comes from steady steps.
