## Body Systems and Energy

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces how your body creates and uses energy. Prompt: What helps your body create energy and stay balanced?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your body has special systems that work together to give you energy. When you eat, breathe, and move, your body uses these systems to turn food into fuel and send it where it’s needed. Taking care of these systems helps you feel strong and focused.

## The Digestive System

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains how food becomes fuel. Prompt: What happens to your food after you eat it?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your digestive system breaks down food so your body can use it for energy. It starts in your mouth and goes through your stomach and intestines. Healthy food and enough water help this system do its job well.

\[interactive element]

## The Circulatory System

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section shows how your body moves nutrients and oxygen. Prompt: How does your heart help your body stay energized?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your circulatory system uses the heart to pump blood. Blood carries oxygen and nutrients from your lungs and food to all parts of your body. A strong heart and clean blood vessels keep your energy flowing.

\[interactive element]

## The Nervous System

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section describes how the brain and nerves control your body. Prompt: How does your brain help you move, think, and react?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Your nervous system includes your brain, spinal cord, and nerves. It sends signals that help you think, move, and feel. Good sleep, balanced food, and calm moments help your brain work at its best.

\[interactive element]

## Systems Working Together

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section emphasizes how body systems are connected. Prompt: How do different parts of your body work as a team to give you energy?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

No system works alone. Your digestive system gets the fuel, your circulatory system delivers it, and your nervous system controls it all. These systems work together like a team to keep you going strong.

## Supporting Your Systems

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section offers ways to care for body systems. Prompt: What habits help your body systems stay healthy?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Healthy choices support your systems. Eating a balanced diet, staying active, getting sleep, and managing stress all help your body stay energized. Little habits make a big difference over time.

\[interactive element]

## Signs of Imbalance

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains how to notice when systems need care. Prompt: What might your body feel like when something’s off?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

When systems are stressed, you might feel tired, dizzy, sick, or moody. These signs are your body’s way of asking for help. Paying attention and getting rest or support can help you get back on track.

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section encourages students to reflect on what works for them. Prompt: What are three things you can do this week to support your energy systems?
\[Prompt Wrap End]
