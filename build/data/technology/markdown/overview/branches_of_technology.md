## Branches of Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces the major branches of technology and their real-world applications.
**Prompt:** Choose one branch of technology and describe a product or career that uses it.
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Technology is a broad field that includes many areas of innovation and practical problem-solving. Major branches include information technology (IT), biotechnology, mechanical and electrical engineering, environmental technology, and more. Each branch applies scientific knowledge to improve human life, industry, and society.

## Information Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores IT and its role in modern communication and systems.
**Prompt:** How does information technology affect your daily life?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Information technology involves computers, software, data, and networks. It powers the internet, mobile devices, cybersecurity, and cloud computing. IT specialists manage systems that run businesses, healthcare, education, and entertainment.

## Biotechnology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section examines how technology interacts with biology and medicine.
**Prompt:** What are some ethical concerns in biotechnology?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Biotechnology uses living systems and organisms to develop products and solutions. It includes genetic engineering, pharmaceutical development, agriculture, and biofuels. It plays a key role in health care, food security, and sustainability.
