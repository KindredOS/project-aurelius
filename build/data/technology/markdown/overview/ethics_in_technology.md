## Ethics in Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces the importance of ethical thinking when designing and using technology.
**Prompt:** What responsibilities do we have when using or creating technology?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Ethics in technology means thinking about right and wrong when developing or using tools. It’s about considering the impact of tech on people, society, and the environment. Good ethics lead to safer, fairer, and more inclusive innovation.

## Privacy and Surveillance

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how data is collected and used—and why consent matters.
**Prompt:** How do you feel about apps or devices tracking your location or behavior?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Modern technology often collects personal information. Ethical tech design asks: Who owns this data? How is it protected? Privacy and surveillance issues raise questions about consent, transparency, and control.

## Bias and Fairness

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section discusses how technology can reflect or reinforce bias.
**Prompt:** Why is it important to make sure algorithms treat everyone fairly?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Algorithms can carry hidden biases if they’re trained on unfair data. Ethical designers work to recognize and reduce bias, ensuring that technology works equitably for people of all backgrounds.

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section looks at how to design tech that works for everyone.
