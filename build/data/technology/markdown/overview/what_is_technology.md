## What is Technology?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores the definition and function of technology in human advancement.
**Prompt**: What is technology, and how is it different from science or nature?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Technology is the application of knowledge to solve problems and improve lives. It includes tools, systems, and methods developed through creativity and engineering. Unlike science, which seeks to understand how the world works, technology focuses on building things that work for us—like machines, software, and infrastructure.
