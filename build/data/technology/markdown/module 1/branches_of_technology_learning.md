## What Are the Branches of Technology?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What do you think the word "technology" includes?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Technology is more than just computers and phones. It includes all the tools and systems people create to solve problems and improve life. From simple machines to space robots, technology is all around us and part of everything we do.

## Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How do you use technology to share ideas or find information?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

ICT focuses on using tools like computers, phones, and the internet to send and receive information. It includes email, websites, video calls, and social media. These tools help people learn, connect, and collaborate across the world.

\[interactive element]

## Medical Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What are some ways doctors and nurses use technology?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Medical technology helps keep us healthy. It includes machines for taking X-rays, software for tracking health records, and robots that assist in surgery. Thanks to technology, doctors can detect and treat illnesses faster and more safely.

\[interactive element]

## Transportation Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How has technology changed the way people travel?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Transportation technology makes it easier to move people and goods. From bicycles to airplanes, GPS systems to electric cars, this branch focuses on speed, safety, and efficiency. It helps us explore, commute, and trade with the world.

\[interactive element]

## Energy and Environmental Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How can technology help protect the planet?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Energy technology includes solar panels, wind turbines, and smart grids that provide cleaner power. Environmental technology helps monitor pollution, clean water, and reduce waste. Together, they support a greener, more sustainable future.

\[interactive element]

## Manufacturing and Construction Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
What kinds of machines or tools help build things?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

This branch includes the tools and processes used to make products and build structures. Robots in factories, 3D printers, and cranes on construction sites all help create the world around us—faster and more precisely than ever before.

\[interactive element]

## Agricultural and Food Technology

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
How do farmers use technology to grow and deliver food?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Technology in agriculture helps farmers grow more food with less effort. Drones, sensors, and smart irrigation systems track crops and weather. Food technology also helps with packaging and preserving food safely for delivery.

## Technology and Your World

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Which type of technology do you find most interesting, and why?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Each branch of technology touches our lives in different ways. By learning about them, you can discover what interests you most—and how you might use technology to solve problems, create new things, or even build your future.
