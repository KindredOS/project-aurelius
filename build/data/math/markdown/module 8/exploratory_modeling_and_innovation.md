### Innovation Through Modeling

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explains how modeling leads to new ideas.  
Prompt: How might math help design a smarter city or a new technology?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Models test possibilities before they become reality.  
- Engineers model bridges before building.  
- Scientists model new medicines.  
- Designers model traffic flow or energy systems.  

Innovation often starts with a mathematical model.
