### What Is Artificial Intelligence?

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** uses algorithms and models to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.  
- Learning from data.  
- Recognizing patterns.  
- Making predictions or decisions.  

At its core, AI is built on mathematical systems.

### Math Behind AI

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explains the foundations of AI models.  
Prompt: Why do you think algebra, probability, and calculus matter in training AI?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

AI relies on:  
- **Linear algebra**: vectors, matrices, and transformations.  
- **Probability and statistics**: handling uncertainty in data.  
- **Calculus**: optimizing models through derivatives and gradients.  

Math powers how AI learns and improves.

### Decision Systems

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces decision-making models.  
Prompt: How can math help a self-driving car decide when to stop or go?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Decision systems use logic, probability, and optimization to guide actions.  
- Algorithms weigh different options.  
- Models predict outcomes of choices.  
- Optimization picks the “best” solution based on goals.  

These systems connect math to real-world actions.

### Ethics and Bias

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section highlights challenges in AI.  
Prompt: Why is it important to check how fair or unbiased an AI model is?  
[Prompt Wrap End]
