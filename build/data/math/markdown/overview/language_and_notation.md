## What Is Mathematical Language?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces math as a language with its own words and symbols. Prompt: Why do you think math has its own special symbols and terms?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Math is like a language—it uses symbols, numbers, and terms to communicate ideas. Just like words in English help us talk, math symbols help us describe patterns, relationships, and problems clearly and quickly.

## Numbers and Operations

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section focuses on how numbers and operations are represented in math. Prompt: What do symbols like +, −, ×, and ÷ tell you to do?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Math uses symbols like + for addition, − for subtraction, × for multiplication, and ÷ for division. These symbols give instructions for what to do with numbers. Learning what each one means is like learning new vocabulary.

## Algebraic Notation

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how variables and equations are written. Prompt: What does a letter like x mean in a math problem?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

In algebra, we use letters to stand in for unknown numbers. This is called a variable. An expression like 2x + 1 uses symbols to show a relationship that can change. This kind of shorthand helps us work with patterns and rules.

## Geometry Symbols

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces symbols used in geometry. Prompt: What do you think the symbols ∠ or ∥ might mean?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Geometry has its own set of symbols, like ∠ for angle or ∥ for parallel lines. These symbols help us describe shapes, positions, and sizes in a universal way that works across languages and cultures.

## Order of Operations

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains the rules for solving expressions in the correct order. Prompt: Why does it matter which part of a math problem you solve first?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Math has a specific order for solving expressions—called PEMDAS (Parentheses, Exponents, Multiply/Divide, Add/Subtract). This rule keeps everyone solving problems the same way and getting the same results.

\[Prompt Wrap Start]

## Interpreting Symbols in Context

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section shows how math symbols change meaning based on how they're used. Prompt: How can the same symbol mean different things in different problems?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Sometimes a symbol can mean more than one thing. For example, the minus sign can mean subtraction or a negative number. It’s important to think about what the symbol means in the situation.
