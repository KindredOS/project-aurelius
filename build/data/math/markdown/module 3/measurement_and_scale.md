## What Is Measurement?

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces measurement as a way to compare and describe the world.  
Prompt: Why do we need units like inches, meters, or minutes?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

**Measurement** tells us how long, tall, heavy, or full something is. Units like inches, meters, pounds, and liters give us a common language. Without units, it would be hard to share or compare information accurately.

## Measuring Length, Area, and Volume

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explains different kinds of measurement.  
Prompt: How is measuring a line different from measuring a box?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

- **Length**: one-dimensional distance (e.g., 5 cm).  
- **Area**: two-dimensional space (e.g., 20 cm²).  
- **Volume**: three-dimensional space (e.g., 100 cm³).  

Each type of measurement helps us describe shapes and objects in different ways.

## Units and Conversions

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section shows why unit conversions are important.  
Prompt: If a bottle holds 1 liter, how many milliliters is that?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Units can change size, but the quantity stays the same.  
- 1 meter = 100 centimeters  
- 1 liter = 1,000 milliliters  
- 1 hour = 60 minutes  

Converting units lets us compare and combine information correctly.

## Scale and Proportion

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces scale as a way to shrink or enlarge objects.  
Prompt: How does a map show a huge place on a small piece of paper?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

A **scale** compares the size of a model to the real thing.  
- Example: On a map, 1 inch might represent 1 mile.  
- Example: A blueprint might use 1 cm to represent 1 meter.  

Scale models and drawings keep proportions accurate while resizing.

## Ratios in Measurement

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explains how ratios connect to scale.  
Prompt: If a toy car is 1/20 the size of a real car, what does that mean?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Ratios help describe relationships in measurement.  
- A scale ratio of 1:20 means the real car is 20 times bigger than the toy.  
- Recipes also use ratios, like 2 cups of water for every 1 cup of rice.  

Ratios and scale ensure fairness, accuracy, and consistency.
