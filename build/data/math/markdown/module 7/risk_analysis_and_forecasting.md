## Risk Analysis and Forecasting Tools

---

### What Is Risk?

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces risk as the chance of loss or harm.  
Prompt: How is deciding to bring an umbrella connected to risk?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

**Risk** combines probability and impact.  
- Probability: how likely something is.  
- Impact: how big the effect would be.  

Mathematics helps us weigh these together to make smart choices.

### Forecasting with Data

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces forecasting as predicting future trends.  
Prompt: How do businesses predict how many products they’ll sell next month?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

**Forecasting** uses past data to predict future outcomes.  
- Line graphs show trends.  
- Regression models fit equations to data.  
- Simulations explore “what if” scenarios.  

Forecasts help guide planning in uncertain conditions.

### Tools for Decision Making

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section connects risk and forecasting to practical choices.  
Prompt: How can math help you choose between two risky options?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

- **Expected value** compares outcomes by averaging probability × payoff.  
- **Decision trees** map out paths and choices.  
- **Sensitivity analysis** tests how results change with assumptions.  

These tools support clear, logical decisions under uncertainty.
