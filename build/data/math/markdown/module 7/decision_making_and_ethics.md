---

### Math in Decisions

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces math as a tool for making choices.  
Prompt: How might equations or data help you decide between two different options?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Math helps compare options by putting them into numbers.  
- Costs, benefits, and probabilities can be calculated.  
- Models highlight trade-offs.  
Quantitative decision-making makes choices more transparent and informed.

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section highlights trade-offs in real-world choices.  
Prompt: How can math show when one group benefits more than another?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Decisions often involve limited resources.  
- Who gets more funding, and who gets less?  
- Which areas get new infrastructure first?  
Math shows impacts clearly, but fairness must also be considered.

### Ethics in Modeling

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explains why ethical thinking matters in math models.  
Prompt: Why is it risky to make decisions only by looking at numbers?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Models simplify reality. If assumptions are biased, results can be unfair.  
- Ignoring certain groups skews results.  
- Overemphasizing cost might ignore human needs.  
Ethics ensures models are used responsibly.

### Transparency and Accountability

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explores responsibility in decision-making.  
Prompt: Why should people explain the data and methods behind their decisions?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Clear communication builds trust.  
- Showing assumptions and methods lets others check the math.  
- Accountability means owning both successes and mistakes.  

Transparency is key for ethical use of data.
