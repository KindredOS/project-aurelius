## What Is Optimization?

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces optimization as finding the best solution.  
Prompt: How do you decide the “best” choice when you have different options?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

**Optimization** means finding the maximum or minimum value of a function to solve a problem.  
- Maximizing profit, area, or speed.  
- Minimizing cost, distance, or time.  

It’s about choosing the most efficient or effective solution.

## Using Equations to Optimize

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section shows how algebra helps with optimization.  
Prompt: How would you maximize the area of a rectangle if the perimeter is fixed?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Optimization problems often turn into equations.  
- If perimeter = 20, rectangle sides can be written as x and (10 – x).  
- Area = x(10 – x) → quadratic function.  
- The maximum occurs at x = 5 (a square).  

Equations reveal where maxima or minima happen.

## Systems Thinking

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section introduces systems as parts working together.  
Prompt: How is a school like a system where each part matters?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

A **system** is made of connected parts that interact. In math, systems of equations or functions represent relationships between variables. Solving them together helps us see the “big picture.”

## Optimization in Systems

[Prompt Wrap Start]  
This section explains how optimization works in multi-variable problems.  
Prompt: How might a company decide how much of two products to make with limited resources?  
[Prompt Wrap End]

Systems can model constraints and trade-offs.  
- Example: Two products share limited materials.  
- Equations and inequalities represent costs, limits, and profits.  
- Graphing or solving simultaneously shows the best combination.  

This is called **linear programming** in advanced math.
