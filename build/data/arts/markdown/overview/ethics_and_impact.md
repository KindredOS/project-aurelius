## Ethics, Ownership & Impact

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: Why is it important to think about where art comes from and who made it?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Art has power. It can inspire, provoke, and influence the world. But with that power comes responsibility. Artists and audiences must think critically about who made a work, where it came from, and what it represents. This awareness helps us respect cultures, ideas, and creative ownership.

## Intellectual Property

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What does it mean to own an idea or a piece of creative work?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Intellectual property refers to creations of the mind—like music, writing, or artwork. Copyright laws help protect artists by making sure they get credit and control over how their work is used. Respecting these rights encourages originality and fairness in creative communities.

## Cultural Appropriation

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What’s the difference between appreciation and appropriation?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Cultural appropriation happens when someone takes ideas or symbols from another culture without understanding or respecting their meaning. It's different from appreciation, which involves learning about and honoring those cultures. Good art builds bridges, not stereotypes.

## Representation in Art

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: Why does it matter who gets to tell stories in art?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Art shapes how we see the world, so it's important that many voices are heard. When people see themselves represented in media and art, it builds pride and belonging. Diversity in storytelling leads to richer, more accurate perspectives.

## Digital Art and Authenticity

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How can we tell if digital art is original or copied?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

With digital art, it can be hard to know who made what. Tools like digital signatures, metadata, and blockchain can help prove originality. Artists must still be careful about giving credit, avoiding plagiarism, and being honest about how they create.

## Social Impact of Art

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: Can art change the way people think or act?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Yes! Art can raise awareness, spark dialogue, and motivate action. From protest posters to climate murals, artists use their voices to shine light on injustice or imagine better futures. Students can explore how their own creative choices have meaning and influence.

## Ethics in Collaboration

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: How do artists work together respectfully?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Collaboration means listening, sharing credit, and being honest about who did what. Artists should set clear agreements and support each other's ideas. Ethical teamwork creates stronger, more inclusive creative communities.

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
Prompt: What do you want your art to say about you and your world?
\[Prompt Wrap End]
