## STEAM Integration

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores how each component of STEAM contributes to creative problem-solving. Prompt: How do science, technology, engineering, arts, and math work together to solve real-world problems?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

STEAM is an educational approach that blends Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics into a unified system. Rather than learning each subject in isolation, STEAM encourages students to apply knowledge across disciplines. Whether designing a bridge, coding a game, or illustrating an ecosystem, integrating these skills helps solve real-world challenges.

## Science in STEAM

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section focuses on the role of science in understanding and addressing challenges. Prompt: What questions can science help you answer when solving a problem?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Science helps us ask and answer questions about the natural world. It builds our understanding of how things work—from the flow of electricity to the growth of plants. Scientific thinking allows us to investigate problems methodically, collect data, and test ideas before acting.

## Technology in STEAM

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces how tools and innovations drive problem-solving. Prompt: What types of technology could help you test or improve your ideas?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Technology gives us the tools to design, test, and share our ideas. From laptops and sensors to 3D printers and virtual labs, tech helps make abstract ideas real. It also allows for faster experimentation, wider collaboration, and creative expression.

## Engineering in STEAM

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section describes the process of designing and building solutions. Prompt: How do engineers turn ideas into real solutions?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Engineering is about using science and math to design, build, and improve things that solve problems. It involves brainstorming, planning, prototyping, testing, and revising. Engineers learn from failure and keep refining their ideas until they work.

## Arts in STEAM

\[Prompt Wrap End]

## Math in STEAM

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains how math helps us measure, model, and analyze. Prompt: Why is math important when building or testing a solution?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Math is the language of patterns, measurement, and logic. It helps us make accurate calculations, build scale models, and interpret data. In STEAM projects, math ensures precision and supports clear thinking about quantities and change.

This section emphasizes the importance of collaboration across STEAM fields. Prompt: Why is teamwork important in STEAM projects?
