## Ethics & Integrity

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces ethics in engineering and why they matter.
**Prompt:** Why should engineers care about honesty, safety, and fairness in their work?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Engineering affects people’s lives. That’s why engineers must act responsibly. Ethics means doing what’s right—even when it’s hard. Engineers must be honest, protect public safety, and make choices that are fair and respectful.

## Safety First

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explains the importance of designing things that keep people safe.
**Prompt:** What could happen if an engineer ignores safety when designing something?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

A broken bridge or a faulty machine can hurt people. That’s why safety is a top priority. Engineers follow strict rules and test their designs to make sure they don’t put people at risk. Good design saves lives.

## Honesty and Transparency

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section highlights why engineers must be truthful in their work.
**Prompt:** Why is it important for engineers to tell the truth about test results or problems?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

## Engineering Disasters

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
