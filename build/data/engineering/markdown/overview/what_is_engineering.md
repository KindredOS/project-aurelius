## What is Engineering?

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces the field of engineering and how it solves real-world problems.
**Prompt:** How is engineering different from science, and what kinds of problems does it help solve?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Engineering is the practical application of science and math to design, build, and improve tools, systems, and structures. Engineers take ideas and turn them into real solutions—like bridges, smartphones, clean energy systems, and medical devices. While science seeks to understand the world, engineering uses that understanding to make life better.

## The Engineering Process

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores the steps engineers follow to create and improve things.
**Prompt:** Think of a time when you solved a problem by trying, testing, and improving something.
\[Prompt Wrap End]

The engineering process involves identifying a problem, brainstorming solutions, designing and building prototypes, testing them, and making improvements. It’s a creative cycle of learning through doing. Failure isn’t the end—it’s part of getting better.

## Branches of Engineering

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces the major types of engineering and what they focus on.
**Prompt:** Which type of engineer would you want to be, and what would you design?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Engineering has many branches. Mechanical engineers design machines, electrical engineers work with circuits and power, civil engineers build infrastructure, and software engineers create apps and programs. There are also aerospace, environmental, biomedical, and many more fields!

## Engineering in Everyday Life

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section highlights how engineering impacts the world around us.
**Prompt:** Look around you—what are five things near you that an engineer helped create?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

From the roads we travel on to the devices we use, engineering touches almost every part of our lives. Engineers design clean water systems, create energy-efficient homes, and invent the tools that keep us safe and connected.

## Innovation and Design Thinking

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section introduces design thinking as a problem-solving method used in engineering.
**Prompt:** How do you think empathy (understanding others' needs) helps in designing better solutions?
\[Prompt Wrap End]

Design thinking focuses on understanding people’s needs, imagining new solutions, and improving them based on feedback. It encourages creativity, collaboration, and empathy—key traits for designing things that truly work for others.

## Engineering and Society

\[Prompt Wrap Start]
This section explores the responsibilities engineers have to society and the environment.
